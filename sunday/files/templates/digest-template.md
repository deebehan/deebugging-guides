# digest-template.md

## Formats
[The summary in the chat, the page, or both.
For the page: masthead name, place, one colour.]

## The summary
1. Greeting. One line, per voice.md.
2. The week label. "Week: Mon 6 to Sun 12 Oct".
3. REMEMBER. Money, forms, bring-and-wear, bookings. Amounts and deadlines
   exactly as written. Things already paid marked done rather than deleted.
4. Day by day. Only days with something on. Each day: who is out, who has
   drop-off and pickup, then what is on, with times exactly as written.
5. RADAR. One line.
6. Sign-off. One line.
7. Read for this run: each calendar by name with its event count, the mail
   label with its message count, and anything that could not be read.

A blank line between each day.
Short enough to copy into a group chat: about 1200 characters, not counting
line 7. When it runs
long, cut commentary, never a time, a place or an owner.
