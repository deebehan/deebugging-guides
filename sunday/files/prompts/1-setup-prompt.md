I am setting up a weekly family message that an AI will write from my calendar
and my school emails. Before that can work I need four things in place, and I
want you to walk me through them. I am not technical.

1. A Google Family group and its Family calendar, which the other adults in my
   house can see and add to.
2. My school's calendar showing inside my Google Calendar, if the school
   publishes one that can be subscribed to.
3. A label in Gmail that catches the school emails (and the club, the
   childminder, whoever sends me dates and bills), so that is where you look.
4. A decision on weather: whether you should look up my local forecast each
   week, and for which town.

HOW I WANT YOU TO WORK WITH ME

- First ask what I am on, phone or computer. Some of this can only be done in
  a browser on a computer. If that is true for a step, tell me before I start
  it, not halfway through.
- Look up Google's current help pages before giving me steps. Menus move. Do
  not describe a button from memory.
- One step at a time. Tell me what to click, then wait for me to say done. Do
  not give me a long list of future instructions.
- If I say I cannot see it, ask what I can see, or ask for a screenshot. Do not
  repeat the same instruction.
- Ask before you assume. I may already have a Google Family group, a Family
  calendar, a school calendar or a school label. If I do, check it is set up
  right rather than making a second one.

GOOGLE FAMILY AND THE FAMILY CALENDAR

- First check whether I already belong to a Google Family group. If I do, look

THE SCHOOL CALENDAR

- Ask me which schools, daycares or clubs publish dates, one at a time.

    a link I can subscribe to (it often says "subscribe", "iCal", "ICS" or

    a PDF or a page on the website, and nothing else
- If I find a link, ask me to paste it and tell me whether it looks like one I

GMAIL LABEL AND FILTER

WEATHER

go on. If you can see the Family calendar but not the school one, tell me
